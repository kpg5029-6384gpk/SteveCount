# Fiishy skin and buddy emotes

## Choose the linked skin

Open **K → Character → Buddy** and cycle to **Fiishy**, then **Save & Done**. Select **Character** or **Both** for Display.

This is the exact 64x64 skin image from the user-selected [NameMC skin b80874f06808211e](https://namemc.com/skin/b80874f06808211e). It is bundled, so selection works offline and does not change your own Minecraft account skin. Steve, Alex and the already-loaded Player skin remain available. See SKIN-NOTICE.md for attribution.

## Emotes

For the rounded non-player model, choose **K → Character → Buddy: Astronaut**. The same emotes and head ride apply. **Personality / sounds** provides optional automatic emotes, noises and volume controls; see [ASTRONAUT.md](ASTRONAUT.md).

Press **J** while playing, or open **K → Character → Buddy options → Emotes / Voice commands**. J is reassignable under Minecraft Controls.

- **Wave:** raises and waves one arm for four seconds.
- **Dance:** alternating arms/legs with a body sway for six seconds.
- **Cheer:** raises both arms and bounces for four seconds.
- **Tiny head rider:** uses a separately adjustable avatar scale (18% by default; J > Buddy options) and seats it on the top of your head. This is a toggle; it remains there as you move, until you turn it off or choose Return to ground.
- **Stop emote / Return to ground:** ends the emote and restores the ordinary follower.
- **Recall:** resets the follower's route near you.

Use **F5** to see the tiny rider. It is hidden in first person to keep it out of the camera. Its count label is hidden while riding to keep your head clear of a large nameplate. Your configured follower size is restored when returning to ground.

Emotes are cosmetic, client-only and do not damage anyone. They have **no TikTok gift bindings** in this release. Pressing an emote button enables character display and animations if necessary. The global Animations switch stops animated emotes; the static head ride still works. A real enabled gift punch takes visual priority and then returns to head-riding mode.

Head-riding preference and skin selection persist across restarts. Temporary emotes stop on leaving a world or dying and are not saved as stream statistics. Other players do not see the buddy; game capture can.
