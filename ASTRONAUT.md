# Rounded astronaut buddy

Open **K → Character** and cycle **Buddy** to **Astronaut**. Select **Character** or **Both** under Display, then **Save & Done**.

This is an original rounded 3D mesh inspired by the supplied astronaut illustration: an oversized dark visor, helmet rim/ear pieces, grey hoodie and drawstrings, blue trousers, white gloves and purple sneakers. It is not a Minecraft player skin. Curved surfaces use smooth vertex normals and Minecraft lighting. The existing Steve, Alex, Player and Fiishy options remain available.

It follows the same route as the other buddies and supports viewer labels, Wave, Dance, Cheer, the normal gift punch animation and tiny head riding. Head riding is still a manual toggle; random activity does not move it onto your head unexpectedly. Use F5 to see it when riding.

## Random emotes and noises

Press **J → Personality / sounds**, or **K → Character → Emotes / Head ride → Personality / sounds**.

- **Random emotes:** occasionally chooses Wave, Dance or Cheer. Manual emotes and gift punches take priority.
- **Random noises:** occasional short original robot-like chirps, boops and warbles. No speech, recordings or external sound services are used.
- **Activity: Calm:** emotes every 35–75 seconds; noises every 50–100 seconds.
- **Activity: Playful:** emotes every 20–45 seconds; noises every 25–60 seconds.
- **Buddy volume:** independent 0–100% slider; default 20%. Minecraft's Master and Friendly Creatures sound sliders also apply. Volume zero is silent.
- **Preview one sound:** plays one sample at the selected volume, even if automatic noises are switched off. Join a world first.
- **Turn off all random activity:** disables both automatic features. You can still use manual emote buttons.

Random emotes and noises default to on, with Calm timing. They only run while a buddy is enabled in an active world and visible through the normal HUD setting. Pausing, hiding the HUD, leaving the world or disabling an option resets its timer; no catch-up burst occurs on resume. The global Animations switch also disables random emotes. Random noises have their own switch.

These are cosmetic, client-only actions. They never enable gift damage, create fake gifts, issue a punch, change health, or send anything to TikTok. They work offline without the companion and do not change the existing Hand Heart gift matcher, ordinary punch damage or pause-menu behavior.

No additional mod dependency is needed. The model, white mesh texture and three short Ogg sounds are bundled in the JAR. The reference illustration is not redistributed. The original geometry, synthesis code and generated tones are authored for SteveCount; the separate Fiishy skin attribution remains in SKIN-NOTICE.md.
